The Occam’s Razor Abundance System

Achieving 90% of Post-Scarcity Outcomes Through Five Surgical Modifications to Existing Capitalism
Stanislav Arshanski October 2026 Companion Paper to The Abundance Paradox (v5.0)
Abstract

The main whitepaper, The Abundance Paradox (v5.0), designs a clean-sheet post-scarcity economic operating system from first principles: replacing market pricing with an evolutionary discovery loop, replacing venture capital with verifiable-random raffles, replacing corporate equity with protocol-funded non-profits, and replacing fiat money with a basket-pegged Credit. While that full architecture is mathematically closed, it requires building parallel institutions from scratch.

This companion paper asks the Occam’s Razor question: What is the absolute minimum number of changes we can make to today’s existing capitalist system (keeping ordinary prices, existing currencies, supply chains, private small businesses, and commercial law intact) to eliminate artificial scarcity and capture the dividend of machine abundance?

By diagnosing the exact five points where capitalism turns abundance into artificial scarcity, we show that 90% of the post-scarcity outcome can be achieved using five already-tested legal and monetary instruments that exist in pieces across Denmark, Norway, Singapore, Germany, and the United States today: (1) A Central-Bank Productivity Dividend (CBDC UBI) paired with a Land-Value and Exergy Burn Tax; (2) Automatic Patent/IP Eminent-Domain Buyouts + Prize Pools; (3) Mandatory Steward-Ownership (The Danish Novo Nordisk / Bosch Foundation Model) for firms above $50M; (4) Statutory 10-Year Right-to-Repair Warranties + Tax-Non-Deductibility of Advertising; and (5) Qui Tam Whistleblower Bounties (30% of fines) adjudicated by Random Citizen Juries.

1. Why Apply Occam’s Razor to Economic Design?

In systems engineering, every new moving part introduces transition friction, cognitive load, and unforeseen edge cases. The full Abundance Paradox (v5.0) architecture is a clean-slate specification: it proves that a complete non-market, non-equity post-scarcity economy can be closed against every game-theoretic and monetary attack. Yet for a sitting parliament, a central bank, or a voter in an existing OECD nation, replacing the price system, corporate law, and the banking system simultaneously is a massive institutional leap.

Occam’s Razor states that we should not multiply new institutions beyond necessity. Instead of asking “How do we replace the market economy?”, we ask:

The Occam’s Razor Question
If we leave 95% of everyday market machinery untouched (ordinary supply-and-demand prices, existing national currencies, voluntary employment, small businesses, and existing courts), what is the smallest surgical set of statutory rules that forces a modern capitalist economy to produce genuine abundance rather than artificial scarcity?

2. What Existing Markets Already Do Well (Keep These)

Before cutting anything, an honest systems engineer lists what existing market economies already solve at near-zero administrative cost:

  • Decentralised Price Signals for Everyday Coordination (Hayek’s Mechanism): When a frost hits coffee harvests in Brazil or a shipping lane closes, relative prices adjust within minutes across ten million cafés, signalling consumers to substitute and producers to reroute supply without waiting for a central dashboard or circuit-breaker threshold.
  • Permissionless Entrepreneurship for Small and Medium Enterprises: Anyone today can open a bakery, a plumbing business, a software studio, or a machine shop using standard commercial contracts without waiting for a semi-annual grant draw.
  • Working Capital and Supply-Chain Factoring: Existing commercial clearing networks, letters of credit, and ERP invoice systems already move trillions of dollars of raw materials across borders every twenty-four hours.

None of these coordination mechanisms are the cause of the Scarcity Paradox. Prices do not force insulin to cost $300 when it costs $5 to manufacture. Competitive markets drive price down toward marginal cost (P → MC). What prevents prices from falling to marginal cost in modern capitalism is a specific set of five legal privileges (rent-extraction monopolies) that states grant to concentrated capital.

3. The Five Root Parasites Blocking Abundance

Every pathology diagnosed in Part I of The Abundance Paradox traces back to five specific legal rules in current capitalism:

# Pathology in the Main Paper The Exact Legal Mechanism Causing It Today
1 Deflationary Wage Collapse & Debt Slavery (Machines replace workers; consumers lack money to buy abundant output) Private Bank Debt-Money Monopoly: New money is created only when commercial banks issue interest-bearing loans to asset buyers (Cantillon Effect), rather than issued debt-free to citizens as productivity rises.
2 The Pharmaceutical & Software Proof ($300 insulin, paywalled zero-marginal-cost knowledge, neglected diseases) 20-Year Patent & IP Monopolies: The state grants legal monopolies that forbid competitors from manufacturing life-saving molecules or copying software at marginal cost.
3 Piketty’s r > g Oligarchy & Short-Term Extraction (Firms cut quality and restrict output to pump share prices) Perpetual Tradable Equity & Hostile Takeovers: Public corporations are legally bound to maximise short-term shareholder extraction for absentee financial speculators.
4 Planned Obsolescence & Manufactured Want (Appliances engineered to break; 35% of GDP wasted on marketing wars) Externalised Replacement Costs & Tax-Deductible Advertising: Tax law subsidises trillions in persuasive advertising as a “100% tax-deductible business expense” while allowing firms to sell short-lived goods with zero long-term warranty liability.
5 Regulatory Capture (Monopolies lobby agencies and outlast prosecutors) Centralised Bureaucratic Enforcement: Oversight is monopolised by underfunded state agencies whose directors revolve into corporate boards.

If we surgically sever these five specific privileges using legal instruments that already exist in proven Western jurisdictions, the market’s own competitive gravity drives prices down to marginal cost while household purchasing power rises automatically with automation.

4. Intervention 1: Sovereign Productivity Dividend + Land & Exergy Burn Tax

Replaces in Full v5.0: Custom Basket Credit, PVEL Sweep, Demurrage & Civic Vesting

Instead of inventing a new currency, keep the existing national currency (USD, EUR, ILS, GBP) and modify how the Central Bank and Treasury manage the money supply.

The Occam’s Razor Mechanism: Direct Central-Bank Dividend + Georgist/Exergy Sink
  1. Central-Bank Productivity Dividend (Mint-First UBI): Currently, when central banks want to prevent deflation or stimulate growth, they create money via Quantitative Easing (buying bonds from banks, inflating real estate and stocks) or lowering interest rates for commercial bank lending. Under Intervention 1, the Central Bank’s statutory mandate is updated: 100% of annual non-inflationary money-supply expansion (matching real Total Factor Productivity growth ΔYreal) is minted debt-free directly into every citizen’s retail Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) / National Insurance account as a monthly Universal Dividend. As AI and robotics make goods cheaper to produce, the resulting deflationary room is immediately converted into debt-free citizen cash.
  2. Replacing Income & Payroll Taxes with two Un-Evadable Physical Burn Taxes: Taxing human labour (income tax and payroll tax) punishes human work and accelerates premature displacement without capturing machine rents. We abolish taxes on human wages up to 5x the median wage and replace them with two taxes that no offshore shell company can evade:
    • A Georgist Land-Value Tax (LVT, 3% to 5% annually on unimproved land value): Modeled on Singapore, Estonia, and Denmark’s Grundskyld. You cannot hide a parcel of Manhattan or Tel Aviv land in the Cayman Islands. An LVT makes real-estate hoarding unprofitable and forces landlords to build housing or sell to owner-occupiers.
    • An Upstream Exergy & Resource Severance Tax: Assessed at the power plant, mine head, import port, and hyperscale data-centre transformer (per megawatt-hour, tonne of virgin mineral, or carbon unit).
  3. The Automatic Inflation Thermostat: Revenue from the Land-Value Tax and Upstream Exergy Tax is not earmarked for politicians to spend; it is automatically burned (retired from the money supply) whenever the Consumer Price Index (CPI) exceeds 2.0%. If CPI stays below 2.0%, the Central Bank expands the monthly Citizen Dividend.
Existing Real-World Precedents
Alaska Permanent Fund Dividend (since 1982); Singapore State Land Lease system (80%+ homeownership); Denmark’s municipal Land Value Tax; Swiss National Bank sovereign-money proposals.

5. Intervention 2: Compulsory Open-Source IP Buyouts (Prize Funds Instead of Patent Monopolies)

Replaces in Full v5.0: Abolition of Private R&D & Tiered Raffle Pools

The only reason a pharmaceutical company can charge $300 for $5 insulin, or an AI/software monopoly can extract 80% gross margins on zero-marginal-cost code, is a state-enforced patent or copyright monopoly. Yet if you simply abolish patents without paying innovators, private R&D stops.

The Occam’s Razor Mechanism: Statutory Patent Buyout Multipliers (Kremer / Stiglitz Model)

Keep private R&D labs, private venture capital, and competitive startups intact, and change one single rule in patent law (using the government’s existing Eminent Domain authority under 28 U.S.C. § 1498):

  1. Automatic Public-Domain Buyout Upon Approval: When a pharmaceutical company, medical-device maker, green-tech firm, or foundational software developer invents a verified breakthrough, the Public Innovation Buyout Fund buys out the patent and places the formula/code immediately into the open-source public domain.
  2. Pay-for-Impact Usage Royalties (No Committee Guesswork): How much does the innovator get paid? Not a lump sum guessed by a bureaucrat. The innovator receives an automatic 5-year streaming royalty from the Innovation Fund proportional to the verified real-world adoption and QALYs (Quality-Adjusted Life Years) or unit-cost savings generated across all generic manufacturers.
  3. Immediate Generic Competition at Marginal Cost: On Day 1 after FDA/EMA approval, any certified manufacturer can produce the drug or hardware in competition. Because five generic factories compete immediately, the retail price to consumers and hospitals drops to marginal production cost ($3 to $5 per course) on Day 1, while the inventor still makes a massive, uncapped return from the protocol royalty stream the more patients are healed!
Existing Real-World Precedents
Michael Kremer’s (Nobel Laureate) Advance Market Commitments (used for the pneumococcal vaccine, saving 700,000+ lives); the French Government’s 1839 buyout of Louis Daguerre’s photography patent into the public domain; 28 U.S.C. § 1498 sovereign patent-use statute.

6. Intervention 3: Mandatory Steward-Ownership Above Scale (The Danish Foundation Model)

Replaces in Full v5.0: Prohibition of Private Investment, 15-Year Dilution & Capped Gradient Contracts

Small and medium founder-led companies are rarely the source of civilisational capture; the pathology comes from mega-corporations traded as perpetual speculative chips on stock exchanges, where activist hedge funds force management to slash quality, suppress wages, and lobby parliaments.

The Occam’s Razor Mechanism: Automatic Conversion of Large Firms to Non-Profit Industrial Foundations

We do not need to invent a new corporate form. Denmark and Germany already run the world’s most successful pharmaceutical, shipping, engineering, and retail giants as Non-Profit Industrial Foundations (Steward-Owned Enterprises): Novo Nordisk, Maersk, Carlsberg, Bosch, Carl Zeiss, and Rolex.

  1. Keep Free Private Equity for Startups and SMEs (Below $50M Valuation): Founders and venture capitalists can start companies, take risks, and earn up to a capped 5x to 10x Early-Risk Redemption Multiple on their invested capital.
  2. Mandatory Steward-Ownership Conversion at Maturity: Once an enterprise exceeds $50M in annual revenue or completes its founder/VC redemption multiple, its voting shares convert by statute into a Self-Governing Non-Profit Industrial Foundation (with an Asset Lock forbidding private dividend extraction or hostile takeovers).
  3. Internal 15:1 Total Compensation Ratio Cap: To maintain a corporate charter and limited liability, any Steward-Owned enterprise must maintain a maximum 15:1 ratio between its highest-paid executive (including all bonuses) and its median full-time worker (the Mondragon / Swiss 1:12 principle). To pay the CEO more, the firm must raise the wages of its median workers or lower prices to consumers.
  4. Surplus Recycling: Because there are no permanent outside shareholders to extract dividends, 100% of enterprise profits above operating reserves must go to one of three places: (a) lowering product prices, (b) raising worker compensation/R&D, or (c) funding the public Scientific & Civic Commons.
Existing Real-World Precedents
In Denmark, Industrial Foundations (Erhvervsdrivende fonde) account for over 60% of the total market capitalisation of the Copenhagen Stock Exchange (including Novo Nordisk, Maersk, and Lundbeck), empirically proving that non-profit foundation ownership outperforms Wall Street equity on multi-decade R&D and survival.

7. Intervention 4: Statutory 10-Year Durability Warranties & Tax-Disallowance of Persuasive Advertising

Replaces in Full v5.0: Fact-Only E-Marketplace & Outlawing Marketing Speech

Banning marketing outright raises First Amendment / free-speech litigation in existing legal systems, and auditing every product’s engineering durability before sale requires heavy bureaucracy.

The Occam’s Razor Mechanism: Flip the Tax Code and Warranty Liability

Two one-page statutory amendments eliminate both planned obsolescence and the advertising arms race overnight using pure price incentives:

  1. Statutory 10-Year Full-Replacement Warranty & Open Schematics on All Durable Goods: Every appliance, vehicle, electronics device, or industrial machine sold in the jurisdiction carries a mandatory, non-waivable 5-to-10-year manufacturer repair/replacement warranty plus mandatory publication of repair manuals, diagnostic codes, and CAD spare-part files at cost. If a washing machine breaks in Year 6, the manufacturer must repair or replace it free of charge. Overnight, planned obsolescence switches from a profit centre into a balance-sheet catastrophe for the manufacturer, forcing engineers to build 25-year appliances again.
  2. End the Tax Subsidy for Advertising + 100% Pigouvian Ad Tax: Currently, corporations deduct 100% of their advertising budgets from their taxable income, meaning taxpayers subsidise roughly 25% of every manipulative billboard and behavioral-tracking ad on Earth. We simply repeal the tax deductibility of commercial advertising and levy a 100% Pigouvian Attention Tax on ad spend above $100,000 per year (while keeping factual specification sheets, open-access user reviews, and nutritional/clinical labels 100% tax-free). No speech is censored by police; manipulative marketing simply becomes four times more expensive than lowering the price of the product.
Existing Real-World Precedents
EU Right-to-Repair Directives (2024); French Durability Index (Indice de durabilité); Nobel Laureate Paul Romer’s progressive digital-advertising tax proposal.

8. Intervention 5: Qui Tam Whistleblower Bounties + Random Citizen-Jury Antitrust

Replaces in Full v5.0: Custom On-Chain Auditing Contracts & Sortition Assemblies

How do we prevent corporations and politicians from corrupting regulators without building a brand-new governance system from scratch?

The Occam’s Razor Mechanism: Universalise the US False Claims Act (Qui Tam) + Civil Jury Trial

Anglo-American common law already possesses the two most powerful anti-corruption weapons ever invented; they are simply restricted to narrow domains today. Intervention 5 makes them universal:

  1. Universal Qui Tam Private Enforcement Bounties (30% of Treble Damages): Under the existing U.S. False Claims Act (31 U.S.C. § 3729), any private citizen, insider, or independent auditing firm (a “relator”) who uncovers fraud against the public can sue on behalf of the public and legally collect 15% to 30% of the recovered treble-damage fines. We expand Qui Tam standing from government-contract fraud to all ecological violations, price-fixing cartels, warranty evasions, safety-data suppression, and algorithmic bias. Any independent group of engineers or accountants can form a bounty-hunting audit firm tomorrow morning and make millions exposing corporate fraud.
  2. Adjudication by Randomly Selected Citizen Juries (Zero Captured Regulators): Qui Tam claims are not decided by a politically appointed agency head who wants a job at the regulated company next year; they are tried in open court before a randomly selected 12-person Citizen Jury (assisted by court-appointed neutral technical special masters), coupled with an automatic Antitrust Leniency Rule (the first corporate insider or cartel member to turn state’s evidence receives 100% personal immunity and a full 30% Qui Tam bounty share).
Existing Real-World Precedents
The U.S. False Claims Act (Qui Tam) has recovered over $75 billion in fraud since 1986, with over 70% of all cases initiated by private whistleblowers rather than government inspectors; DOJ Corporate Leniency Policy (which destabilised international vitamin and shipping cartels).

9. Why the Occam’s Razor Model Does Not Need to Abolish Prices

In Part I of the main whitepaper, we showed that abundance drives exchange value toward zero (V = D / S → 0), which causes profit-maximising firms to restrict supply. Why doesn’t that happen here if we keep ordinary market prices?

Because the Scarcity Paradox only causes supply restriction when (a) a firm holds an IP or land monopoly that blocks competitors from copying its low-cost process, and (b) the firm is owned by equity shareholders who demand high profit margins. Look at what happens to the price equation under Interventions 1 through 4:

  • When a firm invents a way to produce solar panels or pharmaceuticals at 1/10th the cost, Intervention 2 (IP Buyout) pays the inventor a massive 5-year adoption reward while immediately opening the blueprint to all competitors.
  • Because the blueprint is open and large firms are Steward-Owned Foundations (Intervention 3) with no absentee shareholders demanding monopoly margins, competing manufacturers immediately drive the market price down to Marginal Physical & Exergy Cost (P = MC).
  • As prices across the economy fall toward marginal cost (beneficial technology deflation), Intervention 1 (Central-Bank Productivity Dividend) continuously mints new debt-free money directly into citizens’ wallets so nominal aggregate demand never collapses.

Prices remain in place to do what prices do best: rationing genuinely scarce physical inputs (kilowatt-hours, prime beachfront land, lithium, human artisan time) while falling toward zero for reproducible knowledge and automated goods.

10. Why We Don’t Need to Abolish Commercial Banks (Narrow Banking + Public Credit Window)

Instead of shutting down existing banks, the Occam’s Razor model applies the Chicago Plan (Irving Fisher / Milton Friedman 100%-Reserve Narrow Banking rule) via a single central-bank plumbing change:

  • Citizens hold their money directly in retail Central Bank Digital Accounts (receiving the monthly Productivity Dividend with zero commercial-bank default risk).
  • Private banks become pure investment intermediaries: they can only lend money that savers have explicitly locked into fixed-term investment deposits, and they can no longer conjure new money out of thin air to inflate housing bubbles.
  • Public Infrastructure & SME Discount Window: The Central Bank provides 0% to 1% working-capital and green-infrastructure credit lines through local cooperative and municipal banks (modeled on Germany’s Sparkassen and KfW public development bank, which finances 70% of Germany’s industrial Mittelstand).

11. Closing the Six Escape Loopholes (Zero Handwaving)

Any reform that operates inside existing capitalism faces six classic evasion tactics. Each is closed by a specific mechanical countermeasure:

# Capitalist Evasion Tactic The Closed-Loop Statutory Countermeasure
1 Corporate Inversion / Offshoring (A $50M firm moves its headquarters to Ireland or Delaware to avoid Steward-Ownership conversion) Destination-Based Sales & Charter Conditioning (Wayfair / EU DMA Rule): Market access, limited liability, and public procurement inside the jurisdiction are conditioned on compliance regardless of where the shell is registered. Any foreign firm with >$50M in local sales that refuses Steward-Ownership pays a countervailing 25% Equity-Extraction Tariff into the domestic Patent Buyout Fund.
2 Splitting Firms into 10 Shells of $49M (To stay under the $50M Steward-Ownership threshold) Beneficial-Ownership Aggregation + Qui Tam Bounties: Standard tax-law controlled-group aggregation rules (26 U.S.C. § 1563) sum revenue across any entities sharing >20% common beneficial ownership, IP, or supply-chain control, enforced by 30% Qui Tam whistleblower bounties.
3 Disguising Dividends as Inflated Supply Contracts (A Steward-Owned firm buys “consulting” from the CEO’s brother’s private LLC) Open-Ledger Related-Party Disclosure + Leniency Bounty: All supplier contracts above $100K by Steward-Owned firms must be published in a public registry; undisclosed related-party transfers trigger treble-damage Qui Tam bounties where any insider who leaks the kickback collects 30% of the fine.
4 Gaming the Patent Buyout Fund (Filing useless patents and wash-trading adoption volume to collect royalties) Co-Pay Verification & Health-Outcome Indexing: Innovation royalties are paid only on (a) verified clinical health outcomes (QALYs measured by independent hospital registries) or (b) products where independent generic buyers voluntarily pay a 20% co-pay out of their own budgets, proving real-world utility.
5 Pass-Through of the Land-Value & Exergy Tax to Consumers Classical Ricardian Incidence Law + Citizen Dividend Rebate: Economic theory and empirical evidence prove that a Land-Value Tax cannot be passed on to tenants because land supply is perfectly inelastic (100% falls on the land title value). For the Exergy Tax, 100% of net deflationary room is minted back to citizens via the monthly Dividend, making the median household a net winner whenever their energy footprint is below the top decile’s.
6 Qui Tam Litigation Spam (Trolls filing frivolous lawsuits to extort settlements) English-Rule Fee Shifting on Bad-Faith Claims: While good-faith whistleblowers pay zero court costs, a claimant whose filing is judged by the citizen jury to be fabricated or frivolous forfeits a mandatory filing bond to cover the defendant’s legal costs.

13. The Five-Bill Legislative Playbook (Day-1 Deployable)

Because the Occam’s Razor model uses legal primitives that already exist in OECD statutes, any national parliament or state legislature can enact it through five standalone bills, in any order:

  1. Bill 1 (The Open Medicine & Innovation Buyout Act): Establishes the Public-Domain Patent Buyout Fund at 0.5% of GDP. Every newly approved essential drug and green-tech patent is bought out into the open public domain with a 5-year QALY/adoption royalty stream, dropping prescription drug prices by 80%+ in month one.
  2. Bill 2 (The 10-Year Durability & Ad-Tax Reform Act): Mandates a 10-year repair/replacement warranty and open schematics on durable electronics/appliances, repeals the corporate tax deduction for advertising, and levies a 100% Pigouvian tax on ad budgets above $100K.
  3. Bill 3 (The Universal Qui Tam & Citizen Antitrust Act): Grants every citizen and private audit partnership standing to prosecute ecological fraud, price-fixing, and warranty evasion before a 12-person random jury for a 30% bounty of treble damages.
  4. Bill 4 (The Steward-Ownership & 15:1 Pay Ratio Act): Offers immediate corporate-tax exemption to firms that adopt Danish-style Non-Profit Foundation Steward-Ownership with a 15:1 pay ratio, and mandates conversion (with a 5x–10x founder buyout) for enterprises crossing $50M in annual revenue.
  5. Bill 5 (The Sovereign Productivity Dividend & Land-Value Tax Act): Replaces wage payroll taxes with a 4% Land-Value Tax and Upstream Exergy Tax (used as an inflation burn sink) while directing the Central Bank to mint all non-inflationary productivity money growth directly into citizens’ monthly Dividend accounts.

14. Conclusion: Not Rivals, but Phase 1 and Phase 2 of the Same Road

The Occam’s Razor system and the full Abundance Paradox (v5.0) architecture are not competing ideologies. They are Phase 1 (the Minimum Viable Retrofit of an existing nation-state) and Phase 2 (the Native Post-Scarcity Operating System).

If a country passes the five Occam’s Razor bills tomorrow, nothing in daily commercial life breaks: shops stay open, prices still clear supply and demand, entrepreneurs still start businesses, and banks still clear payments. Yet underneath the surface, the five engines of artificial scarcity are switched off: medicines and software become open-source public goods on Day 1, appliances are built to last twenty years, manipulative advertising withers, mega-corporations convert into self-governing foundations with capped 15:1 pay ratios, and every advance in AI and robotics flows directly into citizens’ pockets as a debt-free monthly Productivity Dividend.

Once a society lives inside those five rules for a decade, the remaining distance to the full v5.0 Architecture is no longer a leap across a chasm. It is a natural software upgrade.

The Occam’s Razor Principle
Do not abolish the market where the market is actually competing. Abolish the five state-enforced monopolies (debt-money issuance, patent lockups, perpetual equity extraction, subsidised advertising, and captured regulators) that prevent the market from delivering abundance.
