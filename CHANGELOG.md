- **Mint-First UBA (Reverse-Cantillon Productivity Dividend) & PVEL Burn-Sink Thermostat (§10, §10.1, §10.2, §34.9, §34.16):** Upgraded the monetary architecture from tax-and-spend to a **Mint-First + Burn-Sink Thermostat**:
  - **Mint-First Productivity Expansion:** The protocol mints UBA and Raffle Credits debt-free directly into citizens' streams and project escrows to monetize real productivity growth ($\Delta Y_{\text{RealProductivity}}$), solving the Day-1 liquidity bootstrap paradox and eliminating the Sin-Tax Paradox (when companies use 50% less energy/minerals, real productivity rises so the UBA Mint Rate goes UP even as resource tax burn goes DOWN).
  - **Two-Part PVEL Burn Sink (§10.1):** Company charges operate as pure physical and monetary thermostats where 100% of collected Credits are permanently burned: (Sink A) 100% automated net surplus burn at e-marketplace settlement (enforcing the non-profit mandate and draining spent UBA liquidity), and (Sink B) upstream exergy, compute, and uniform-price finite resource quota auctions (§34.9) burned at the physical bottleneck source.
  - **Master Monetary Thermostat Equation & PID Controller (§10.2):** Governed by $M_{\text{issued}}(t) = [B_{\text{SurplusSweep}}(t) + B_{\text{ResourceUsage}}(t) + B_{\text{Demurrage}}(t)] + \Delta Y_{\text{RealProductivity}}(t) - \gamma \cdot \pi_{\text{basket}}(t)$, keeping consumption-basket inflation locked at $0.0\%$.
- **Silvio Gesell Demurrage (§10.2):** Added a 2.5% annual algorithmic decay on idle Credit balances above 2x the annual consumption basket per person, forcing monetary velocity and making shadow-lending or hoarding negative-carry.
- **Dual-Circuit Export-Backed FX Clearinghouse (§10.2, §23):** Domestic non-tradables clear 100% in internal Credits; external imports clear through an Export FX Buffer Pool fed by zero-IP, zero-marketing-margin exports. Tier-1 essential inputs clear at 1:1 par; Tier-2 discretionary imports pay a dynamic FX congestion surcharge.
- **Working-Capital Credit Lines vs. Capex Raffles (§11.2):** Separated new entry/R&D (funded via the Open Raffle) from ongoing factory/hospital operations (funded via revolving, zero-interest Protocol Clearing Credit Lines collateralized by verified e-marketplace orders and audited Bills of Materials).
- **Day-1 Sovereign Lifetime Consumption Annuities (§22):** Eliminated backward-induction capital flight and asset stripping during the 15-year equity phase-out by allowing legacy physical asset owners to convert productive plants on Day 1 into non-transferable Lifetime Consumption Annuities, strictly conditional on intact, audited handover of the physical facility.
